This is the entry point for using pera1. To get something running, follow "Installation" and "Quickstart" in order. If you want to understand how it works before designing your app, read "Concepts" first. It will make the later guides easier to follow.

## Three pages to read first [#first-three]

1. [Installation](/getting-started/installation) — Add the packages and check the prerequisites.
2. [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart) — Build a contacts app while trying out route definitions, loaders, actions, and navigation.
3. [Concepts](/getting-started/concepts) — Get the big picture of route matching, history entries, and data flow.

## Then look things up by task [#after-that]

Everything after this is reference material. You don't need to read it in order. When you're stuck or know which feature you want to build, open just the page you need.
